Budgets — Baselines and Forecasts

Purpose

Define budget constructs, versioning, hierarchy, and allocation models.

Structure

- Budget links to Project and optionally to a Contract.
- Components: labor, vendor, equipment, render, storage, travel, software, overhead, contingency.
- Budget lines may be allocated to sequences/shots/tasks or to cost centers.

Versioning

- Baseline (approved) vs Revised Baseline vs Forecast.
- Each approved baseline is versioned and preserved.

Reporting

- Budget vs Actual vs Forecast reports required at project and org level.

Next steps
