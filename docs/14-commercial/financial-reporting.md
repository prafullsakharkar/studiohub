Financial Reporting — Recommended Reports & Snapshots

Purpose

Describe the baseline reports and snapshots StudioHub should provide for finance and production stakeholders.

Recommended reports

- Project P&L (Revenue, Costs, Gross Margin) — baseline vs actual vs forecast
- AR Aging (Outstanding invoices by aging buckets)
- AP Aging (Vendor invoices outstanding)
- Budget vs Actual (by component and rollup)
- Committed vs Actual vs Forecast (include POs and approved commitments)
- Time Utilization (billable vs non-billable hours by role)
- Vendor Performance (on-time, quality, cost variance)
- Cashflow Forecast (expected inflows from invoices and outflows from payments/POs)

Snapshot & Exports

- Exportable CSV/Excel for accounting system handoff
- Fiscal period snapshots for audit trails

Next steps

- Design report APIs and scheduled snapshot exports.
- Work with finance to confirm KPIs and frequency.
