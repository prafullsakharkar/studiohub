Utilization — Metrics & Definitions

Purpose

Define utilization metrics and separate them from billability and productivity.

Key metrics

- Available Hours (per period)
- Allocated Hours (planned/committed)
- Actual Hours (time entries)
- Utilization Rate = Allocated Hours / Available Hours
- Realized Utilization = Actual Hours / Available Hours

Guidance

- Distinguish utilization for planning (allocated) and realized (actual).
- Billability is a separate metric derived from Actual Hours classified as billable.
- Provide time-windowed metrics (weekly, monthly) and department/skill breakouts.
