Vendor Capacity — Teams & Confirmations

Purpose

Define how vendor-provided capacity is modeled and integrated into staffing and procurement.

Model

- vendor_team_id
- vendor_id
- capacity_estimate (hours per period)
- confirmed_allocations (PO-linked commitments)
- contact info
- performance metrics

Guidelines

- Model vendor capacity at team-level; avoid requiring vendor employees to have full StudioHub accounts unless necessary.
- Treat vendor confirmed allocations as committed capacity only after PO approval and vendor acknowledgement.

Next steps

- Define a minimal API for vendor capacity proposals and confirmations.
