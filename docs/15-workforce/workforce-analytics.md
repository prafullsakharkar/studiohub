Workforce Analytics — Dashboards & Reports

Purpose

Outline the recommended dashboards and reporting primitives for workforce monitoring and decision support.

Recommended dashboards

- Project Staffing Dashboard: required vs assigned vs unfilled roles, upcoming availability
- Department Capacity Dashboard: capacity, allocations, utilization, skill gaps
- Executive Workforce Dashboard: total capacity, contractor ratio, top risks
- Utilization Trends: historical utilization and realized utilization by role/skill
- Skill Gap Heatmap: demand vs available skill capacity across planning horizon

Reporting primitives

- Capacity snapshots (daily/weekly)
- Allocation history
- Utilization timeseries
- Resource history (assignments, skills, certifications)

Privacy & aggregation

- Aggregate and anonymize sensitive fields for cross-org or executive reports when required.
