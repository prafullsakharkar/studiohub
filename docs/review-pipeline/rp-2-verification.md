## 3. Corrections and choices (also in PLAN.md and CONTRACTS.md)

1. **Notes are not in the change log, and RP-2 does not add them.** The plan said `create_note` was audited; it was not (`apps/audit/signals/change_tracking.py` tracks a fixed list). Adding notes would put their subjects and bodies in change-log rows. Those rows are readable with `audit.view`, also by members bound to a show or a department, who cannot open the notes. **Proposed follow-up**, for you to decide: track notes in the change log only once its readers are held to whole-project access for note rows, or leave the bodies out of the diff.
2. **Narrowed members never reach the notes routes** (404, whole-project access), as before RP-2. Q-RP-13 = A therefore holds without any change. The link check still applies narrowing, which is tested on the validator.
3. **Link codes and names are read live**, so a rename shows. Archived records still answer.
4. **Error answers** keep the contracted field messages and also carry the API's usual `error` object.
5. A reply is stored with `subject` `Re: <thread subject>` and the thread's category. A `PATCH` of a reply naming anything but `body` is 400.
